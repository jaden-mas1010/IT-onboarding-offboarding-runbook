# Offboarding a Departing Employee (Manager Guide)

**Audience:** People managers
**Last updated:** 2026-08-19

When a member of your team is leaving, timely notice to IT ensures a smooth, secure transition and protects company data.

## What to do

1. **Submit an offboarding ticket as soon as the departure date is confirmed.** For planned departures, please give IT at least 2 business days' notice where possible. For immediate/involuntary departures, contact IT directly — same-hour processing applies.
2. **Tell us if any data handoff is needed.** If the departing employee owns files, shared mailboxes, or SharePoint sites that need to be reassigned, flag this in the ticket so we can hand off ownership before access is revoked.
3. **Confirm the device return process.** For remote employees, IT will arrange courier pickup. For office-based employees, please coordinate an in-person handoff on the last day.

## What IT handles automatically

- Disabling account sign-in and revoking active sessions
- Removing MFA methods
- Converting the mailbox to a shared mailbox (accessible to you for 30 days, if needed)
- Removing the employee from all groups, distribution lists, and Teams channels
- Wiping and reclaiming the device once returned

## What you don't need to do

You don't need to manually remove the employee from Teams channels, shared drives, or distribution lists — this is handled centrally by IT to ensure nothing is missed.

## Timeline

For planned departures, all access is revoked by end of the employee's last working day. For immediate departures, access is revoked within the hour of IT receiving notice, regardless of when the device is physically returned.

## Questions?

Reach out to the IT team directly if you're unsure whether something falls under standard offboarding.
