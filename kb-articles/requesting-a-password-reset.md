# Requesting a Password Reset

**Audience:** All employees
**Last updated:** 2026-08-19

## If you know your current password but want to change it

1. Go to **myaccount.microsoft.com** and sign in.
2. Select **Password** from the left menu.
3. Follow the prompts to set a new password.

Your new password must be at least 12 characters and include a mix of upper and lower case letters, a number, and a symbol.

## If you're locked out and can't sign in

1. On the sign-in screen, select **Forgot my password**.
2. Verify your identity using the MFA method you registered (authenticator app or SMS).
3. Set a new password when prompted.

If self-service reset isn't available on your account, or you don't have access to your MFA method:

1. Submit a ticket via the IT helpdesk, or ask your manager to submit one on your behalf if you can't access email.
2. Include your full name and department in the ticket — this speeds up identity verification.
3. IT will reset your password and contact you through an alternate channel (personal email or phone) to share the temporary password securely. We will never send a password over email or Teams chat.
4. You'll be prompted to set a new password on next login.

**Target resolution time:** within 1 business hour during core hours (10:00 AM–3:00 PM ET).

## Why we don't just email you a new password
Sending passwords over unencrypted channels is a security risk, especially for a company handling healthcare data. Verifying identity through a secondary channel protects your account and the organization.
