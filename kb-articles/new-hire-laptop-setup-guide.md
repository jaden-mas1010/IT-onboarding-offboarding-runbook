# New Hire Laptop Setup Guide

**Audience:** New employees, first day
**Last updated:** 2026-08-19

Welcome aboard. Your laptop has already been configured with the core apps and security settings you need — this guide walks you through the first-login steps.

## Step 1: First login

1. Power on your device.
2. Sign in using the temporary username and password provided by IT (sent to your personal email before your start date, or handed to you with the device).
3. You'll be prompted to set a new password immediately. Choose something you haven't used elsewhere.

## Step 2: Set up multi-factor authentication (MFA)

1. You'll be prompted to set up MFA on first login.
2. We recommend the Microsoft Authenticator app (available on iOS and Android) over SMS — it's faster and more secure.
3. Scan the QR code shown on screen with the app and follow the prompts.

## Step 3: Confirm your core apps

Your laptop comes with the following pre-installed:

- Microsoft 365 Apps (Outlook, Word, Excel, Teams, PowerPoint)
- VPN client (for remote access to internal systems)
- Endpoint protection (runs in the background, no action needed)

Open Outlook and Teams to confirm your mailbox and channel access are working. If either shows an error, contact IT immediately.

## Step 4: Access your team's shared resources

Your manager or IT will have already added you to the relevant Teams channels and SharePoint sites for your department. If you can't find a resource you expect to have access to, submit a ticket with the resource name and your department.

## Having trouble?

Submit a ticket through the IT helpdesk or reach out during core hours (10:00 AM–3:00 PM ET) for the fastest response.
